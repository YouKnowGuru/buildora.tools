## Home Inspection Cost Calculator — Pricing Methodology & Reference Guide
*Fee Structure, Add-On Services, and Industry Standards*

---

## What This Calculator Does

This **home inspection cost calculator** estimates the total price of a professional home inspection based on the five factors inspectors actually use when they quote a job: home size, property type, age, foundation type, and regional labor costs. It also prices the most common add-on services — radon testing, mold and air-quality sampling, sewer scope, termite (WDI) inspection, and well water testing — plus rush scheduling.

The estimates follow the flat-fee tier structure published by most American inspection firms and align with the Standards of Practice of the **American Society of Home Inspectors (ASHI)** and the **International Association of Certified Home Inspectors (InterNACHI)**.

---

## 1. How Home Inspection Fees Are Structured

Most inspectors quote a **base flat fee by size tier**, then adjust for complexity:

```
Base Fee (size tier) × Property Type × Age Factor × Foundation Factor × Regional Index
+ Add-On Services + Rush Premium = Total Estimate
```

| Size tier (living area) | Typical national base fee |
|---|---|
| Up to 1,000 sq ft | $200 – $275 |
| 1,001 – 2,000 sq ft | $275 – $400 |
| 2,001 – 3,000 sq ft | $375 – $500 |
| 3,001 – 4,000 sq ft | $475 – $625 |
| Above 4,000 sq ft | $550 + ~$0.09 per sq ft |

The national planning midpoint for a typical 1,500–2,500 sq ft single-family home lands around **$340–$425**, consistent with published Angi/HomeAdvisor/America's Inspector averages.

## 2. Property, Age & Foundation Adjustments

- **Condos and townhouses** run roughly 15% below the detached-house rate: no roof, limited exterior, smaller footprint. Verify whether the association covers the building envelope.
- **Multi-family properties (2–4 units)** cost substantially more — often 40–50% above the single-family rate — because each unit adds a kitchen, bath, panel, heating unit, and egress path to evaluate.
- **Homes 50+ years old** commonly add 15–25%: obsolete knob-and-tube or aluminum wiring, galvanized supply piping, and layered roofing all take extra time to document safely.
- **New construction** adds about 10% for punch-list verification — mis-vented baths, untorqued panel lugs, and missing flashing are routine findings even in builder walk-throughs.
- **Crawlspaces and basements** add structural, moisture, and insulation evaluation time (+6–12%).

## 4. Add-On Service Pricing

| Service | Typical range | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Radon testing | $125 – $275 | Continuous monitor, 48+ hour dwell; EPA action level 4.0 pCi/L |
| Mold / air quality | $250 – $650 | Air + surface samples with accredited lab analysis |
| Sewer scope | $125 – $300 | Camera inspection of the lateral; strongly advised for homes 20+ years |
| Termite / WDI | $75 – $150 | Written wood-destroying insect report; often required for VA loans |
| Well & water | $100 – $300 | Flow test plus basic potability panel (bacteria, nitrates) |
| Rush scheduling | +20 – 30% | Same/next-day booking premium |

A failed sewer lateral can cost $3,000–$25,000+ to repair — the single reason many agents treat the $175 scope as the highest-value add-on in the industry.

---

## 5. What a Standard Inspection Includes

A standard inspection is a **visual, non-invasive** assessment of: roof, flashing, gutters; exterior siding, trim, grading, and drainage; foundation and structure; attic, insulation, and ventilation; electrical panel, wiring, and GFCI sampling; plumbing supply, drains, and water heater; HVAC equipment operation; interior surfaces; and built-in appliances. The written report typically arrives within 24–48 hours.

It does **not** include destructive testing, code-compliance certification, appraisal, or the specialized services listed above unless separately ordered.

## 6. Working With the Estimate

1. Select the size, type, age, foundation, and region closest to the property.
2. Toggle the add-ons your lender, climate, or home age makes advisable.
3. Compare the low/mid/high range with 2–3 written quotes from local inspectors.
4. Use the **custom base fee** override once you have a real quote — the add-ons and schedule estimate still apply.

### How accurate is this calculator?

The midpoint reflects national tier pricing published by inspection firms and industry surveys in 2025–2026; individual quotes vary with travel, detached structures (garages, guest houses), and inspector credentials (CMI, PE-licensed inspectors charge more).

### Should I skip the inspection on a new build?

No. Independent third-party inspections of new construction routinely surface punch-list defects before the builder's warranty deadline, and many buyers stage them at pre-drywall and final walkthrough.
