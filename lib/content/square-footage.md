## Square Footage Calculator — The Complete Estimating & Sizing Guide

## How to Calculate Square Footage (Formulas by Shape)

Square footage is the standard measurement of two-dimensional surface area used throughout the United States, Canada, and the UK for construction, flooring, painting, real estate appraisal, roofing, landscaping, and tiling. In metric countries, the equivalent metric unit is **square meters (m²)**.

Calculating exact square footage ensures you never overspend on materials or run out mid-job. Below are the geometric formulas for every standard shape:

---

### 1. Rectangles and Squares (Most Common Rooms)

For bedrooms, living rooms, hallways, walls, and standard rectangular yards:

> **Square Footage (sq ft) = Length (ft) × Width (ft)**

* **Example:** A room measuring 15 ft long by 12 ft wide has an area of 15 × 12 = **180 sq ft**.
* **If measuring in inches:** Multiply length by width in inches, then divide by 144 (Length in inches × Width in inches ÷ 144).

---

### 2. Circular Areas (Patios, Round Pools, Fire Pits)

For circular patios, round lawns, Gazebos, or tree rings:

> **Area = π × r² = π × (Diameter ÷ 2)² ≈ 3.14159 × r²**

* **Perimeter (Circumference):** C = π × Diameter
* **Example:** A circular patio with a 14 ft diameter has a radius of 7 ft. The area is 3.14159 × 7² = **153.94 sq ft**.

---

### 3. Triangular Areas (Gables, Corner Yards)

For attic gables, triangular yard plots, or diagonal room cutouts:

> **Area = 0.5 × Base (ft) × Height (ft)**

* **Example:** A triangular wall section with a 16 ft base and 10 ft height equals 0.5 × 16 × 10 = **80 sq ft**.

---

### 4. Trapezoidal Areas (Non-Parallel Rooms & Driveways)

For driveways that flare out, or rooms with angled walls:

> **Area = ((Base 1 + Base 2) ÷ 2) × Height**

* **Example:** A driveway with a 16 ft street entrance (Base 1), a 10 ft garage entrance (Base 2), and a length of 20 ft (Height) equals ((16 + 10) ÷ 2) × 20 = **260 sq ft**.

---

### 5. L-Shaped & Complex Multi-Room Layouts

For open-concept living rooms, L-shaped hallways, or complex floor plans:

1. Divide the shape into separate, non-overlapping rectangular sections (Section A and Section B).
2. Calculate the square footage of Section A (Length 1 × Width 1).
3. Calculate the square footage of Section B (Length 2 × Width 2).
4. Add the sections together: **Total Area = Area A + Area B**.

---

## Waste & Overage Factors: How Much Extra Should You Buy?

When purchasing flooring, tile, sod, or drywall, purchasing only the exact measured square footage will leave you short. Material is lost during cutting, staggering joints, fitting around door jambs, pattern matching, and transit damage.

| Material & Layout | Recommended Waste Factor | Key Considerations |
|---|---|---|
| **Hardwood / Laminate (Straight Lay)** | **5% – 10%** | End cuts, staggering plank joints, defect culling |
| **Herringbone / Diagonal Tile or Wood** | **15% – 20%** | Extensive 45-degree angle cuts along all walls |
| **Ceramic & Porcelain Tile (Grid)** | **10% – 12%** | Wet saw cuts, corner fits, transit breakage |
| **Luxury Vinyl Plank (LVP / LVT)** | **7% – 10%** | Click-lock end trims and door jamb undercuts |
| **Carpet (Broadloom Rolls)** | **10% – 15%** | Roll width mismatch (12' or 15'), pile direction |
| **Grass Sod / Turf** | **5% – 10%** | Curved garden beds, edge trimming |
| **Drywall (Sheetrock)** | **10% – 12%** | Window/door cutouts, joint layout |

---

## How to Calculate Boxes of Flooring or Tile Needed

Most flooring and tile products are packaged in boxes containing a fixed square footage (typically 15–25 sq ft per box). 

> **Boxes to Purchase = Ceil(Required Area with Waste ÷ Coverage per Box)**

* **Example:**
  * Exact Room Area: **153.94 sq ft**
  * With 10% Waste: **169.33 sq ft**
  * Box Coverage: **20 sq ft / box**
  * **Boxes Needed** = 169.33 ÷ 20 = 8.47 → **9 boxes**
  * **Actual Total Coverage:** 9 × 20 = **180 sq ft** (leaving 10.67 sq ft extra for future repairs).

## Square Footage to Other Unit Conversions

| Conversion | Formula | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Square Feet to Square Yards (yd²)** | Sq Ft ÷ 9 | 180 sq ft ÷ 9 = **20 sq yd** (Carpet) |
| **Square Feet to Square Meters (m²)** | Sq Ft × 0.092903 | 1,000 sq ft × 0.0929 = **92.9 m²** |
| **Square Meters to Square Feet (sq ft)** | m² × 10.7639 | 100 m² × 10.764 = **1,076.4 sq ft** |
| **Square Feet to Acres** | Sq Ft ÷ 43,560 | 43,560 sq ft = **1.0 Acre** |
| **Square Inches to Square Feet** | Sq In ÷ 144 | 1,440 sq in ÷ 144 = **10.0 sq ft** |
