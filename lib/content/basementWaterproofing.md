## Basement Waterproofing Cost Calculator

Use this basement waterproofing cost calculator to create a practical planning estimate for a wet or leaking basement. Select the waterproofing approach, describe the moisture issue, and add the work your project needs—such as a sump pump, crack repair, or excavation.

## Typical Basement Waterproofing Costs

| Solution | Typical planning range |
|---|---:|
| Interior waterproof coating | $3–$7 per sq ft |
| Interior French drain | $50–$100 per linear ft |
| Exterior membrane | $70–$140 per linear ft |
| Exterior drain tile | $100–$175 per linear ft |
| Sump pump installation | $800–$2,000 |
| Foundation crack repair | $350–$1,500+ |

Interior systems are generally the least disruptive way to manage water after it enters the foundation. Exterior systems address water before it reaches the wall, but digging beside the foundation can add substantial labor, landscaping restoration, and access costs.

## What Affects the Price?

The largest driver is usually the number of foundation-wall feet being treated. A small basement may still have a long perimeter, and that perimeter controls drain, membrane, and excavation work. Active leaks and hydrostatic pressure can also require a more robust drainage plan than minor surface dampness.

Sump pumps, discharge piping, battery backups, wall crack injection, finished-basement demolition, difficult access, utility conflicts, and rebuilding patios or landscaping are common costs that may not appear in a basic quote. Ask each contractor whether permits, disposal, and restoration are included.

## Interior vs. Exterior Waterproofing

Interior drains collect water at the footing and route it to a sump basin. They are often a good fit when exterior access is limited. Exterior membranes and drain tile are most effective when the foundation can be excavated safely and water is entering through the wall or footing joint. A full system combines drainage with wall protection for persistent water problems.

## Important Note

This calculator is for budget planning, not a diagnosis. Standing water, foundation movement, mold, electrical hazards, or a suddenly worsening leak warrant an on-site assessment by a qualified waterproofing or foundation professional.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does it cost to waterproof a basement?

Many projects fall between $3,000 and $12,000, but an exterior system with excavation can exceed that range. The wall length, access, soil conditions, and amount of water management needed matter more than basement floor area alone.

### Is interior waterproofing cheaper?

Usually. It avoids excavation, but it manages water after it reaches the foundation. The right choice depends on where the water originates and whether outside access is practical.

### Does a sump pump solve basement leaks?

A sump pump removes collected groundwater; it does not seal wall cracks or correct all surface drainage problems. It is commonly one part of a drainage system.
