## Siding Calculator — Complete House Siding Estimating Guide

## How Much Siding Do I Need? Step-by-Step Calculation Method

Estimating exterior siding accurately is essential for homeowners, general contractors, and siding installers. Ordering too little material causes costly project delays, extra freight charges, and the risk of color mismatch from different manufacturing batches. Ordering too much wastes hundreds of dollars in non-returnable materials and disposal fees.

In the North American siding and construction industry, siding is measured and sold in **squares**. 

> **1 Siding Square = 100 Square Feet (9.29 m²)**

Whether installing vinyl lap siding, James Hardie® fiber cement planks, LP® SmartSide® engineered wood, traditional cedar shingles, or architectural metal panels, the fundamental geometric formulas remain consistent.

---

## Step 1 — Calculate Gross Exterior Wall Area

For standard rectangular structures (colonial, ranch, split-level, and modular homes), exterior walls form a perimeter around the foundation footprint:

- **Gross Wall Area (sq ft)** = Perimeter (ft) × Wall Eave Height (ft)
- **Perimeter (ft)** = 2 × (Building Length + Building Width)

### Multi-Story Buildings & Measurement Basis
- **Total Wall Height Basis:** Measure from the foundation sill plate directly to the upper roof soffit/eave line.
- **Per-Story Basis:** If measuring single-story wall height (e.g. 9 ft per floor), multiply by the number of above-grade stories (9 ft × 2 stories = 18 ft total wall height for a 2-story colonial).

* **Worked Example:** A single-story ranch home measuring 40 ft long by 30 ft wide with a standard 9 ft wall height:
  * **Perimeter** = 2 × (40 + 30) = **140 linear ft**
  * **Gross Rectangular Wall Area** = 140 × 9 = **1,260 sq ft**

---

## Step 2 — Add Gable End Areas (Triangular, Gambrel, & Arched)

On pitched roofs, the triangular or arched wall sections extending above the eave line up to the ridge peak must be sided.

| Gable Geometry | Geometric Formula | Standard Architectural Application |
|---|---|---|
| **Triangular Gable** | Area = 0.5 × Base Width × Peak Height × Count | Standard Gable, A-Frame, & Cross-Gable Roofs |
| **Gambrel Gable (Dutch Barn)** | Area = [((Base + Break Width) / 2 × Lower Height) + (0.5 × Break Width × Upper Height)] × Count | Dutch Colonial, Barns, & Mansard Roofs |
| **Half-Circle / Arched Gable** | Area = 0.5 × π × (Base Width / 2)² × Count | Victorian, Barrel Vault, & Romanesque Accents |

* **Example (continued):** The ranch home has 2 standard triangular gable ends, each with a 30 ft base and a 5 ft peak height:
  * **Gable Area** = 0.5 × 30 × 5 × 2 = **150 sq ft**
  * **Total Gross Surface Area** = 1,260 + 150 = **1,410 sq ft**

> 💡 **Note on Hip Roofs:** Hip roofs slope downward toward all four exterior walls and do not have gable wall ends. For hip roofs, select **"No gables / Flat or Hip roof"** (or set Gable Count to 0).

---

## Step 3 — Deduct Openings (Doors, Windows & Garages)

Siding is not installed over windows and doors. Subtract all wall openings to determine the **Net Siding Area**.

| Opening Category | Standard Dimensions | Typical Square Footage |
|---|---|---|
| **Standard Entry Door** | 3 ft × 7 ft (36 in × 84 in) | **21.0 sq ft** (1.95 m²) |
| **Patio French / Sliding Door** | 6 ft × 7 ft (72 in × 84 in) | **42.0 sq ft** (3.90 m²) |
| **Standard Double-Hung Window** | 3 ft × 4 ft (36 in × 48 in) | **12.0 sq ft** (1.11 m²) |
| **Large Picture Window** | 5 ft × 5 ft (60 in × 60 in) | **25.0 sq ft** (2.32 m²) |
| **Single-Car Garage Door** | 9 ft × 7 ft (108 in × 84 in) | **63.0 sq ft** (5.85 m²) |
| **Double-Car Garage Door** | 16 ft × 7 ft (192 in × 84 in) | **112.0 sq ft** (10.40 m²) |

- **Net Siding Area = Gross Wall Area − Total Opening Areas**

* **Example (continued):** The home has 2 exterior entry doors (42 sq ft), 8 standard windows (96 sq ft), and 1 single garage door (63 sq ft):
  * **Total Opening Deductions** = 42 + 96 + 63 = **201 sq ft**
  * **Net Siding Area** = 1,410 − 201 = **1,209 sq ft**

---

## Step 4 — Apply Waste & Cutting Overage Factor

Every siding installation produces scrap from cutting panels around windows, starter strips, corners, and gable angles. Adding an overage factor guarantees adequate material to complete the job.

- **Total Siding to Purchase** = Net Siding Area × (1 + Waste % / 100)
- **Siding Squares Required** = Total Siding to Purchase (sq ft) / 100

| Siding Material | Recommended Waste Factor | Rationale & Job Site Conditions |
|---|---|---|
| **Vinyl Lap Siding** | **5% – 10%** | Flexible, easy to cut, standard 12' lengths with minimal end loss |
| **Metal / Steel / Aluminum** | **5% – 10%** | Pre-formed panels with clean straight shear cuts |
| **Fiber Cement (James Hardie®)** | **10% – 12%** | Heavier, brittle planks requiring dustless saw cuts; off-cut loss |
| **Engineered Wood (LP® SmartSide®)** | **10% – 12%** | Rigid composite planks; joint spacing over 16" o.c. studs |
| **Cedar Clapboard / Bevel Wood** | **12% – 15%** | Natural wood knot trimming, grain matching, grading culls |
| **Wood Shakes & Shingles** | **15% – 20%** | Extensive overlap, staggered exposures, irregular widths |
| **Complex Architectural Facades** | **+5% extra** | Dormers, bay windows, multi-level eaves, turret walls |

* **Example (continued):** Applying a standard 10% waste factor:
  * **Waste Buffer** = 1,209 × 0.10 = **120.9 sq ft**
  * **Total Siding to Order** = 1,209 + 120.9 = **1,329.9 sq ft**
  * **Siding Squares Required** = 1,329.9 / 100 = **13.3 Squares** (Order 14 squares from distributor)

---

## Step 5 — Calculate Packaging: Boxes, Cartons & Bundles

Siding is shipped in standardized cartons or bundles:
- **Vinyl Lap Siding:** Typically packaged in **2-square cartons (200 sq ft)**.
- **Fiber Cement (HardiePlank):** Sold in pallets or bundles (e.g. 1/3 square or ~33 sq ft per bundle).
- **Cedar Shingles:** Sold in bundles (4 bundles per square = 25 sq ft/bundle).

- **Cartons to Purchase** = Ceil(Total Siding Area with Waste / Coverage per Box)

* **Example:** 1,329.9 sq ft needed / 200 sq ft per carton = 6.65 → **7 cartons** (1,400 sq ft actual coverage, leaving 70.1 sq ft extra for future repairs).

---

## Step 6 — Accessory & Trim Breakdown

In addition to siding planks, complete exterior installations require dedicated trim components:

1. **Starter Strip:** Fastened along the bottom chalk line of the entire foundation perimeter.
   * **Starter Strip LF** = Building Perimeter = **140 linear ft**
2. **Outside Corner Posts:** Installed vertically on all exterior corners.
   * **Corner Post LF** = Outside Corners (4) × Wall Height (9 ft) = **36 linear ft**
3. **J-Channel & Trim:** Needed around all 4 sides of windows, 3 sides of doors, and along gable roof rake lines.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is a "Square" in siding?
In construction, one square is equal to exactly **100 square feet** of surface area (10 ft × 10 ft). Siding materials (vinyl cartons, fiber cement pallets, cedar bundles) and contractor labor estimates are priced by the square.

### How do I estimate siding for a 1,500 sq ft vs 2,500 sq ft house?
A house's siding square footage depends on its **exterior wall surface area**, not its interior floor space. A single-story 1,500 sq ft home (30 × 50 ft footprint, 9 ft eaves) has approximately 1,440 sq ft gross wall area (approx. 12–14 squares net with waste). A two-story 2,500 sq ft home (35 × 36 ft footprint, 18 ft total wall height) has approximately 2,550 sq ft gross wall area (approx. 24–27 squares net with waste).

### How do I calculate gambrel (barn-style) roof siding?
A gambrel gable features two distinct pitches: a lower steep slope and an upper shallow slope. Calculate the lower section as a trapezoid: (Base + Break Width) / 2 × Lower Height, and the upper section as a triangle: 0.5 × Break Width × Upper Height. Our calculator computes gambrel geometry automatically.

### Should I deduct all window and door areas?
Yes, deducting doors, windows, and garage openings over 10 sq ft provides an accurate net wall area and prevents ordering excessive siding. Our calculator subtracts standard openings automatically while allowing custom dimensions for precision.
