## How to Calculate Demolition Cost

The calculator uses this industry-standard formula:

```
1. Identify Structure Type → Select the closest match to your building.
2. Measure Total Footprint Area (sq ft or sq m)
3. Enter Number of Stories (debris volume scales with height)
4. Choose Demolition Method:
   - Mechanical (excavator/backhoe) — fastest, lowest cost per sq ft
   - Manual (hand tools, sledgehammer) — 65% more labour-intensive
   - Selective (deconstruction for salvage) — 40% premium, preserves materials
5. Labor Cost = Area × Base Cost/SF × Method Multiplier
6. Debris Volume (cu yd) = Area × Debris Factor × Stories ÷ 27
7. Dumpster Loads = Debris Volume ÷ 30 CY (capacity per roll-off)
8. Haul Cost = Number of Dumpsters × $550 per load (avg)
9. Total Cost = Labor + Haul + Permit Fee
```

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does it cost to demolish a 1,500 sq ft house?
A 1,500 sq ft wood-frame house typically costs **$8,000 – $16,000** for full demolition (labor only). Adding debris hauling ($2,000–$4,000) and a permit ($300–$800) brings the total to roughly **$10,000 – $20,000**. Masonry structures can run 50–80% higher.

### What is the cheapest demolition method?
**Mechanical demolition** (using an excavator or bulldozer) is almost always the cheapest per square foot because it dramatically reduces labour hours. Manual demolition can cost 60–80% more in labour for the same square footage.

### Do I need a permit to demolish a house?
Yes, in almost all US jurisdictions you need a **demolition permit** before tearing down any structure. Costs typically range from $200 to $1,000+ depending on your municipality and structure size. Some areas also require asbestos or lead paint inspections before issuing a permit.

### How long does house demolition take?
A typical 1,500–2,000 sq ft wood-frame house can be mechanically demolished in **1 to 3 days** using an excavator. Manual or selective deconstruction may take 1 to 2 weeks for the same structure.

### What happens to the debris after demolition?
Demolition contractors typically haul debris to a licensed construction and demolition (C&D) landfill. Concrete, steel, and clean wood may be recycled separately. Hazardous materials (asbestos, lead paint) require specialised disposal and can add $2,000–$30,000+ to project cost.

### Is asbestos testing required before demolition?
Federal EPA NESHAP regulations require an accredited asbestos inspection before demolition of any commercial or industrial building, and most states require it for residential structures as well. If asbestos-containing materials (ACM) are found, licensed abatement contractors must remove them before demolition begins.

### How much does interior demolition cost?
Gut-out interior demolition (removing walls, flooring, drywall, fixtures while keeping the shell intact) averages **$2 – $4 per sq ft**, or roughly $3,000 – $12,000 for a typical home gut renovation project.

### What is the cost to demolish a garage?
A detached garage of 400–600 sq ft typically costs **$1,500 – $4,000** for demolition labour, plus $400–$800 for debris hauling. Total garage demolition cost is usually **$2,000 – $5,000** depending on materials and location.
